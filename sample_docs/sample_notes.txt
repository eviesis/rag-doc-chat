Company Onboarding Notes

Our engineering team uses a trunk-based development workflow. All feature
branches are short-lived and merged into main within two days. Code review
requires at least one approval before merging.

Deployment pipeline: commits to main trigger a CI build via GitHub Actions,
which runs unit tests, linting, and a security scan. On success, the build
is pushed to a staging environment automatically. Production deploys require
manual approval from a team lead.

On-call rotation runs weekly, Monday to Monday. The on-call engineer is
responsible for triaging incoming alerts within 15 minutes during business
hours, and within 30 minutes outside business hours.

New hires are expected to complete the onboarding checklist within their
first two weeks, which includes setting up local dev environment, reading
the architecture overview doc, and shadowing one on-call shift.
